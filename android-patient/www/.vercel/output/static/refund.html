Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last Updated: November 2026

✓ Fair & Transparent: If a doctor or hospital cancels consultation or is unavailable, patients receive a 100% full refund.

1. Appointment Cancellation by Patient

Patients can cancel an appointment up to 2 hours before the scheduled doctor consultation window. In such cases, the hospital consultation token fee is refunded to the original payment source (UPI/Card/NetBanking) within 5 to 7 business days.

2. Doctor Unavailability or Hospital Cancellation

If a doctor becomes unavailable due to an emergency or if the hospital cancels the OPD schedule, the full amount (both token fee and platform fee) will be refunded automatically to the patient's original payment method, or the patient may choose to reschedule at no additional charge.

3. Refund Processing Timeline

Approved refunds are processed via our RBI-authorized banking partners (Cashfree/Razorpay). The funds typically reflect in the patient's bank account within 3 to 7 business days depending on the customer's bank settlement cycle.

4. Grievance & Refund Support

If you experience any billing discrepancies, double charges, or pending refunds, please email support@carepath.health with your Token Number and Transaction Reference ID. Our team investigates and resolves disputes within 24 to 48 hours.
