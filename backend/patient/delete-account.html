🗑️ Account & Data Deletion Request

In compliance with Google Play Store policies and global privacy regulations, CarePath provides users full control over their personal data.

What Happens When You Delete Your Account:

  • Your patient profile (name, email, phone number, demographics) is permanently deleted.
  • Uploaded prescription scans and medical queries are permanently erased from our processing servers.
  • Active hospital inquiry messages and chat logs are deleted immediately.
  • Any previous OPD token receipts are anonymized for tax and hospital auditing compliance.

Request Deletion:

Alternative Methods:

1. In-App Deletion: You can directly delete your account anytime from within the CarePath App by navigating to Profile → Account & Privacy Settings → Delete My Account.

2. Email Request: You may also email our data protection officer directly at privacy@carepath.health with the subject "Account Deletion Request". Requests are processed within 24–48 hours.
