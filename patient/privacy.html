Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2026

1. Overview

CarePath respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data in compliance with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP Act) and applicable healthcare regulations.

2. Data We Collect

  • Personal Information: Full name, phone number, email address, and patient demographics.
  • Appointment Details: Selected hospital, department, doctor, and date of consultation.
  • Transaction Data: Payment transaction ID and status via secure payment aggregators (we do not store your credit card or UPI PINs).

3. How We Use Your Data

Your information is used strictly to create and allocate your OPD token number, send SMS/Email booking confirmations and OTPs, calculate queue waiting times, and facilitate hospital reception check-ins.

4. Data Sharing & Security

We only share necessary appointment details with the specific hospital or clinic you have booked with. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal health data to any third-party advertisers. All network transmissions are encrypted with secure TLS/HTTPS protocols.

5. Your Rights & Grievance Officer

You may request correction or deletion of your account data at any time by contacting our Privacy and Grievance Officer at privacy@carepath.health.
